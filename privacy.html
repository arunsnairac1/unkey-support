Privacy
policy.

UnKey · Updated 28 October 2026

In short: UnKey does not collect, share or transmit any of your data. Everything happens on your device, and anything it keeps stays on your device.

Your files and passwords

PDFs you open in UnKey and the passwords you enter are processed only on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. They are never uploaded, sent to a server or shared with anyone. Unlocked copies are kept in the app's temporary storage until you save or share them, and are deleted when you start over or the system clears temporary files.

Saved passwords

If you choose to save a PDF password, UnKey stores it in your device's Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Saved passwords stay on that one device: they are not synced to iCloud, backed up to other devices, or sent anywhere. The names you give them are stored in the app's local settings. Deleting a saved password, or deleting the app, removes it.

Scans, signatures and text recognition

Photos you scan, signatures you draw and the text UnKey reads from pages (to make scans searchable or to find text to redact) are processed on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Your signature is stored only on your device, so you can reuse it. None of this leaves your device.

No servers

UnKey has no servers and never sends your files, passwords or scans anywhere. All of its tools work with no internet connection at all.

UnKey Pro purchases

If you buy or restore UnKey Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. UnKey only receives Apple's confirmation that Pro is active on your Apple Account; we never see your payment details.

No data collection

UnKey has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code. We collect no personal information, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.

This website

unkeypdf.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from third parties. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Because UnKey collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@unkeypdf.app.
