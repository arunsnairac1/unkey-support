Privacy Policy

UnKey · Updated 27 October 2026

In short: UnKey does not collect, share or transmit any of your data. Everything happens on your device, and anything it keeps stays on your device.

Your files and passwords

PDFs you open in UnKey and the passwords you enter are processed only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded, sent to a server or shared with anyone. Unlocked copies are kept in the app's temporary storage until you save or share them, and are deleted when you start over or iOS clears temporary files.

Saved passwords

If you choose to save a PDF password, UnKey stores it in your iPhone or iPad's Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Saved passwords stay on that one device: they are not synced to iCloud, backed up to other devices, or sent anywhere. The names you give them are stored in the app's local settings. Deleting a saved password, or deleting the app, removes it.

No network access

UnKey makes no network connections. It works with no internet connection at all.

No data collection

UnKey has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code. We collect no personal information, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers.

Children

Because UnKey collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
