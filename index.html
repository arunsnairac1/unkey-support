How do I remove a password from a PDF?

Open UnKey, tap Choose PDF and pick the file. You can also use Open in UnKey from the share sheet in Files, Mail or Safari. Enter the PDF's password, then tap Save to Files or Share. The saved copy opens without a password.

I don't know the password. Can UnKey unlock it?

No. UnKey is not a password cracker. You need the correct password to remove protection from a PDF that requires one to open.

My PDF opens without a password but won't let me print or copy.

Open it in UnKey. It removes printing, copying and editing restrictions automatically, with no password needed.

Is my original file changed?

No. UnKey always creates a separate unlocked copy and never modifies the original.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Never. UnKey works entirely offline on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
