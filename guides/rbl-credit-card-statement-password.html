RBL Bank Credit Card Statement Password (2026)

RBL Bank · India · Updated October 2026

First four letters of your name followed by your date of birth in DDMMYY format.

DocumentPasswordStatus

Source

As of October 2026, RBL Bank's published rule for credit card statements is the first four letters of your name followed by your date of birth in DDMMYY format. For Kavita Rao, born 9 November 1988, the password is KAVI091188. The exact format is also stated in the email your statement arrives with.

The password format

RBL Bank's e-banking FAQ, in its credit card section, says that after you download a month's statement you "enter the password (First four letters of Name and DOB in the format of DDMMYY)." The bank's own example uses capital letters: a customer named Nilesh Kapoor born on 1 March 1973 would use NILE010373.

That FAQ dates from 2020, when RBL linked credit cards to its e-banking portal. We found no newer RBL page with a different rule, but formats do change, so treat the statement email as the final word.

Worked example

Kavita Rao was born on 9 November 1988. Her details are made up.

  • First four letters of her name: KAVI
  • Date of birth as DDMMYY: 09 + 11 + 88 = 091188
  • Password: KAVI091188

Note the six-digit date. Many other banks use only DDMM, so if you are used to those, it is easy to leave off the year.

If the password doesn't work

  • Include the two-digit year. RBL's format is DDMMYY, not DDMM and not DDMMYYYY.
  • Use capitals. RBL's own example is in upper case. If capitals fail, try lower case once, then check the email.
  • Leading zeros. A birthday on 9 November is 0911, not 911.
  • Name as per bank records. Use the first four letters of the name RBL has for you, which is usually the name printed on the card. If you have an initial first, try it as part of the four letters.
  • Co-branded cards. RBL issues many co-branded cards. If the statement email for your card states a different rule, follow the email.
  • Get a fresh copy. Statements can be downloaded in the RBL MyCard app or RBL e-banking. Customer care can confirm the date of birth on record.

Keep the statement safe

Your RBL statement shows your spending, credit limit and part of your card number. The password is built from details many people know, such as your name and birthday, so treat it as light protection only. RBL Bank will never ask you for your statement password, card PIN or OTP over a call or message; anyone who does is not the bank.

Questions

What is the RBL credit card statement password?

RBL Bank's FAQ says it is the first four letters of your name followed by your date of birth in DDMMYY format, for example KAVI091188.

Does the RBL password use the full year?

No. RBL's format uses a two-digit year (DDMMYY), so 1988 becomes 88.

Should the letters be capitals?

RBL's own example uses capital letters, so type the four letters in upper case.

Where can I download my RBL card statement again?

In the RBL MyCard app or RBL Bank e-banking, or ask customer care to resend it to your registered email.
