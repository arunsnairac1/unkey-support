IndusInd Bank Statement Password Format (2026)

IndusInd Bank · India · Updated October 2026

First four letters of your name in capitals followed by your date of birth as DDMM, or your 8-digit Customer ID.

As of October 2026, IndusInd Bank's statement emails say the password is the first four letters of your name in capitals, followed by your date of birth as DDMM. For Ravi Kumar, born 15 May 1990, that is RAVI1505. You can also use your 8-digit Customer ID instead.

The exact format is also stated in the email the statement arrived with.

The password format

IndusInd Bank publishes its statement email templates on its own website. Both the monthly statement of account email and the NRI statement of account email say:

  • "Your 8 character password is a combination of first 4 characters of your name and your date of birth in the DDMM format."
  • The password is shown in UPPER CASE only and is case-sensitive.
  • "As an alternate option, you can also use your 8 digit Customer ID as your password."

The bank's own examples include Naresh Kumar, born 15 September, giving NARE1509, and Raj Mathur, born 25 February, giving RAJM2502. The second example shows that when your first name is shorter than four letters, you carry on into the next part of the name.

Worked example

Ravi Kumar, born 15 May 1990, Customer ID 12345678.

  1. First four letters of the name, capitals: RAVI
  2. Date of birth as DDMM: 1505
  3. Password: RAVI1505, or 12345678 using the Customer ID

For Om Das, born 3 July: the letters are OMDA and the password is OMDA0307.

Only the day and month are used. The year of birth is not part of the password, so RAVI15051990 would be rejected. A statement you download yourself from IndusInd net banking or the IndusMobile app may follow a different rule; if so, it is usually shown on screen at the time of download.

If the password doesn't work

  • Capitals. The password is case-sensitive, so check that Caps Lock or your phone keyboard is giving capitals.
  • Leading zeros. 3 July is 0307.
  • Name as per bank records. Use the name the bank has on file, which may start with a surname or initial.
  • Try the Customer ID. All 8 digits, no spaces.
  • Business accounts. Non-individual accounts may use other details; read the email that came with the statement.
  • Credit card statements are different. See our IndusInd credit card statement guide.
  • Still stuck? The statement emails list IndusInd phone banking numbers, and the bank's FAQ page covers other help options. IndusInd will never ask for your password, PIN or OTP.

Questions

What is the IndusInd Bank statement password?

The first four letters of your name in capitals followed by your date of birth as DDMM, for example RAVI1505. Your 8-digit Customer ID also works.

Is the IndusInd statement password case-sensitive?

Yes. The bank's statement emails say the password is case-sensitive and show it in upper case only.

My first name has only three letters. What do I use?

Carry on into the next part of your name. IndusInd's own example is Raj Mathur, born 25 February, giving RAJM2502.

Is the format the same for NRI accounts?

Yes. IndusInd's NRI statement email uses the same name plus DDMM format, with the 8-digit Customer ID as an alternative.
