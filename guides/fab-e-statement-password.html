FAB Account e-Statement Password Format (2026)

First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) · United Arab Emirates · Updated October 2026

Last four digits of your FAB account number followed by your date of birth as DDMM, making an 8-digit password (e.g. 12340102).

Source

As of October 2026, First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) says your account e-statement password is the last four digits of your account number followed by the day and month of your date of birth (DDMM). That makes 8 digits. FAB's own example: account 101XXXXX1234 and birth date 01-02-1989 give 12340102.

The password format

FAB's Help and Support page answers "What is the password to view my account e-statement attachment?" with:

  • Part 1: the last 4 digits of your account number
  • Part 2: your day and month of birth, two digits each (DDMM)

There are no letters, spaces or separators, and the year of birth is not used. The bank describes it as "the 8-digit password".

Worked example

Mariam Haddad (made-up) has a FAB savings account ending in 5678. She was born on 23 September 1991.

  • Last 4 digits of the account: 5678
  • Day and month of birth: 2309

Her password is 56782309.

Watch the order of the date. In the UAE the day comes first, so 3 April is 0304, not 0403. Single-digit days and months need a leading zero.

If the password doesn't work

  • Check which account the statement is for. If you have several FAB accounts, each statement uses the last four digits of its own account number. The masked number on the statement email usually shows them.
  • Use the date of birth the bank has on record. If your passport and the bank's records differ, the bank's record is what counts.
  • Credit card statements may differ. FAB's published answer is for account e-statements. For a card statement, read the instructions in the email it arrived with.
  • Read the email. Formats change, and the email that carries the statement normally explains how to open it.
  • Open in a proper PDF reader. Some email apps' built-in previews don't handle protected PDFs well. Try Adobe Acrobat Reader, Apple Books/Files on iPhone, or Preview on a Mac.
  • Still stuck? Contact FAB through the phone number or chat listed on its help page. FAB will not ask for your Online Banking password.

Questions

What is the FAB e-statement password?

The last four digits of your account number followed by your day and month of birth (DDMM), for example 12340102.

Does the FAB password include my year of birth?

No. FAB's format uses only the day and month of birth, so the password is 8 digits long.

I have more than one FAB account. Which digits do I use?

Use the last four digits of the account the statement belongs to.

Is the FAB credit card statement password the same?

FAB's published answer covers account e-statements. For card statements, follow the instructions in the statement email.
