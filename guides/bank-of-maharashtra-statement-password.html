Bank of Maharashtra Statement Password (2026)

Bank of Maharashtra · India · Updated October 2026

Read in: English · हिन्दी · മലയാളം · தமிழ் · తెలుగు

Bank of Maharashtra statements are reported to open with the first 4 letters of your name in lowercase followed by your date of birth in DDMM.

DocumentPasswordStatus
Savings/current account e-statementFirst 4 letters of name (lowercase) + date of birth in DDMM

Source

As of October 2026, Bank of Maharashtra (BOM) statement PDFs are reported to open with the first 4 letters of your name in lowercase followed by your date of birth in DDMM (day and month only). Pooja Deshmukh, born on 9 November, would type pooj0911.

The password format

Bank of Maharashtra does not publish the PDF password rule on its website, so the formats on this page come from independent guides that agree with each other. Treat them as commonly reported, not officially confirmed. The email or message that delivered your statement states the exact format, and it is always the one to trust.

Easemoney and ScanPilot both describe the same 8-character rule: four lowercase letters, then four digits.

PartWhat to typeExample
NameFirst 4 letters, lowercasepooj
Date of birthDDMM, no year0911

Worked example

Pooja Deshmukh (made up), born 9 November 1992:

  1. First 4 letters of the name, lowercase: pooj
  2. Day and month of birth: 0911
  3. Password: pooj0911

Someone born on 5 March types 0503 for the date part, keeping the leading zeros.

If the password doesn't work

  • Name as per bank records. The letters come from the name BOM holds. If your account is in the name "R Chandak", the first four characters may be different from your first name.
  • No year. Use DDMM only, not DDMMYYYY.
  • Lowercase. pooj0911, not POOJ0911.
  • Short names. ScanPilot reports that names under 4 letters are padded with x ("Om" becomes omxx); check the statement email if this applies to you.
  • Customer ID. ScanPilot mentions the Customer ID as an alternative some statements use.
  • Joint accounts. The primary holder's name and date of birth are reported to be used.
  • Read the statement email, which states the format, or ask your branch.

Questions

What is the Bank of Maharashtra statement password?

It is reported to be the first 4 letters of your name in lowercase followed by your date of birth in DDMM, like pooj0911. Confirm in the statement email.

Do I include my year of birth?

No. The reported format uses only the day and month (DDMM).

Should the letters be capital?

No. The reported format uses lowercase letters.
