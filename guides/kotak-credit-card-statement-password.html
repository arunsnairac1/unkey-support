As of October 2026, Kotak Mahindra Bank's own credit card statement guide says the PDF password is usually your date of birth in MMYYYY format. For Sunil Mehta, born 14 June 1991, that would be 061991. Kotak says "usually", and other formats are reported, so the email your statement arrived with is the best check.

The password format

Kotak's guide to viewing and downloading your credit card statement (published in Hindi on kotak.bank.in) says: "सामान्यत: आपकी जन्मतिथि, MMYYYY फॉर्मेट में PDF का पासवर्ड होता है", meaning "Usually, your date of birth in MMYYYY format is the PDF password."

MMYYYY is month then four-digit year, with no day. That is unusual among Indian banks, and the word "usually" means it may not apply to every card or every statement. We could not find an English Kotak page that repeats it.

Third-party guides describe a different format for Kotak card statements: the first four letters of the name on the card plus your date of birth as DDMM, some saying in lower case. Kotak does not confirm this on its website, so treat it as a fallback to try, not a fact.

Worked example

Sunil Mehta was born on 14 June 1991. His details are made up.

  • Month of birth: 06
  • Year of birth: 1991
  • Password per Kotak's guide: 061991
  • If that fails, the commonly reported alternative: SUNI + 1406 = SUNI1406, then suni1406

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the statement email. Kotak's statement email normally states the password rule. Because the formats reported for Kotak vary, this matters more than usual.
  • Leading zero on the month. June is 06, not 6.
  • Do not keep guessing. Try the two formats above, then stop and check the email or ask the bank.
  • Download without the email. Kotak's help page on checking your credit card bill explains how to view statements in Net Banking (Credit Card > Statement) and the Kotak mobile app.
  • Date of birth on record. If the bank has a different date of birth on file, a date-based password will not match. Customer care can confirm what is on record.

Questions

What is the Kotak credit card statement password?

Kotak's own guide says it is usually your date of birth in MMYYYY format, such as 061991 for June 1991. The statement email states the exact rule for your card.

Does the Kotak password include my name?

Kotak's guide does not mention your name. Some third-party guides report a name-plus-DDMM format, but Kotak does not confirm it.

Where can I download my Kotak card statement?

In Kotak Net Banking under Credit Card > Statement, or in the Kotak mobile app.

Can I change the Kotak statement password?

We found no Kotak option to set your own statement PDF password. You can save an unlocked copy after opening it instead.
