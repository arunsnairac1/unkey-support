IDBI Bank Statement Password Format (2026)

IDBI Bank · India · Updated October 2026

IDBI account statements are commonly reported to open with your Customer ID; IDBI says its credit card email statements open with the first 4 letters of your name in capitals followed by your date of birth (DDMM).

As of October 2026, IDBI Bank account statements are commonly reported to open with your Customer ID, typed as digits only. If Priya Sharma's Customer ID is 12345678, her statement password would be 12345678. IDBI credit card statements are different: IDBI says they open with the first 4 letters of your name in capitals plus your date of birth as DDMM, for example PRIY0902.

The password format

Savings and current account e-statements

IDBI Bank does not publish the account statement password on its website. Most guides report it as your Customer ID, the 8 to 11 digit number that identifies you as a customer (not your account number). A few sources mention date of birth instead. Because we could not confirm this on an IDBI page, treat the Customer ID as the likely format and check the email the statement arrived with, which normally states the rule.

Credit card email statements

IDBI's Euphoria credit card usage guide says: "Your password for email statements will be a combination of the first 4 characters of your name (in capitals) as it appears on your IDBI Bank Credit Card followed by date of birth in DDMM format." That guide is written for the Euphoria card; if you hold another IDBI card, check that the statement email gives the same rule.

Worked example

Priya Sharma was born on 9 February 1988. Her Customer ID is 12345678 and her credit card shows the name PRIYA SHARMA. All details are made up.

  • Account statement: 12345678
  • Credit card statement: first 4 letters PRIY + day and month 0902 = PRIY0902

If her card said P SHARMA, the first four characters would come from that printed name, so check the card rather than guessing.

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the email. The statement email is the best guide to the current format for your account.
  • Customer ID vs account number. They are different numbers. Your Customer ID is shown in your passbook, welcome kit and net banking profile.
  • Capitals matter for cards. The credit card password is case-sensitive: PRIY0902 works, priy0902 does not.
  • Short names. If your name on the card has fewer than four letters before a space, follow the instruction in the statement email.
  • DDMM only. The card format uses day and month, not the year, with leading zeros.
  • Still stuck? Contact IDBI Bank customer care or your branch to confirm your Customer ID and date of birth on record. The bank's internet banking FAQ covers login and profile questions.

Questions

What is the IDBI Bank account statement password?

It is commonly reported to be your Customer ID, digits only, but IDBI does not publish this on its website. Check the email the statement came with for the exact rule.

What is the IDBI credit card statement password?

IDBI says it is the first 4 characters of your name in capitals as printed on your card, followed by your date of birth in DDMM.

Is my Customer ID the same as my account number?

No. The Customer ID identifies you and is printed in your passbook and net banking profile; the account number identifies one account.

Is the IDBI statement password case-sensitive?

For credit card statements, yes: the name letters must be in capitals. The Customer ID is all digits.
