Everest Bank e-Statement Password Format (2026)

Everest Bank Limited · Nepal · Updated October 2026

Your 4-digit birth year, then your 2-digit birth month, then the last 3 digits of your account number, e.g. 198507157.

As of October 2026, Everest Bank says the e-statement password is your 4-digit birth year, your 2-digit birth month, and the last 3 digits of your account number, all together with no spaces. For example, Sita Shrestha (made-up), born 1992/11/08, with an account ending in 482, would type 199211482.

The password format

Everest Bank's e-statement page says savings, current and overdraft account holders can receive monthly statements by email in PDF format. The password is:

  1. Birth year, all four digits (for example 1985)
  2. Birth month, two digits (for example 07 for the 7th month)
  3. The last three digits of your account number

The bank's own example: account 0080-0501-200157 with birth date 1985/07/25 in the system gives 198507157. The day of birth is not used.

Statements are sent once a month to the email address you registered with the bank. If they stop arriving, check with your branch that your email address is still current, and look in your spam or junk folder.

Worked example

Sita Shrestha (made-up):

  • Birth date in the bank's system: 1992/11/08, so the year and month are 199211
  • Account number: 0123-0456-789482, so the last three digits are 482

Password: 199211482.

Two more made-up examples:

  • Born 2001/03/19, account ending 905: 200103905
  • Born 1978/12/02, account ending 060: 197812060. Keep the zero at the start of 060.

If the password doesn't work

  • Use the birth date in the bank's system. Everest Bank says the password uses the birth date held in the system. Nepal uses both the Bikram Sambat (BS) and Gregorian (AD) calendars. If one doesn't work, ask your branch which date and calendar are on your record.
  • Two-digit month. July is 07, not 7.
  • Last three digits only. Use just the final three digits of the account number the statement is for, ignoring dashes.
  • Several accounts. Each statement uses the last three digits of its own account, so passwords differ between accounts.
  • Joint or business accounts. The bank's page doesn't say whose birth date is used for joint accounts or what applies to companies. Check with your branch.
  • Read the email the statement came with, or contact Everest Bank customer care.

Keeping the statement private

The password is built from your birth year and month and part of your account number. Family members or anyone who has seen your cheque book could work it out. Don't forward statements to shared inboxes. If you keep copies on your phone, store them somewhere only you can open.

Removing the password so you don't have to type it again

Questions

What is the Everest Bank e-statement password?

Your 4-digit birth year, 2-digit birth month and the last 3 digits of your account number, for example 198507157.

Does the Everest Bank password use my day of birth?

No. It uses only the year and month of birth, followed by the last three digits of the account number.

Which accounts get e-statements by email?

Everest Bank says savings, current and overdraft account holders can receive monthly statements by email in PDF format.

Should I use my BS or AD birth date?

Use the birth date in the bank's system. If you're not sure which calendar the bank recorded, ask your branch.
