Bank of Baroda Statement Password Format (2026)

Bank of Baroda · India · Updated October 2026

As of October 2026, reports of the Bank of Baroda (BoB) statement password differ. The format two independent guides agree on is your Customer ID; your registered mobile number and first 4 letters of your name in lowercase plus DDMM are also reported. If Neha Joshi's Customer ID is 123456789, she would try 123456789 first.

The password format

Bank of Baroda does not publish the PDF password rule on its website, so the formats on this page come from independent guides that agree with each other. Treat them as commonly reported, not officially confirmed. The email or message that delivered your statement states the exact format, and it is always the one to trust.

FormatExampleReported by
Customer ID123456789Easemoney, ScanPilot
Registered mobile number9876543210ScanPilot (as its main format)
First 4 letters of name, lowercase + DDMMneha2207Easemoney (as its main format)

Easemoney leads with the name rule and gives the Customer ID as the alternative. ScanPilot leads with the mobile number, gives the Customer ID as the fallback, and says the name rule did not open the statements it tested. Because the reports differ, the statement email is the authority for your file.

Worked example

Neha Joshi (made up) has Customer ID 123456789, mobile 9876543210 and was born on 22 July 1990. She tries, in turn:

  1. 123456789
  2. 9876543210
  3. neha2207

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the statement email. Bank of Baroda's covering email or the download screen usually states the format.
  • Customer ID vs account number. They differ. The Customer ID is in your passbook and the bob World app.
  • Self-downloaded statements from bob World or Baroda Connect are sometimes not protected.
  • BOBCARD credit card statements use a separate name-and-date format, so the account password will not open them.
  • Still stuck? Ask your branch to confirm the format.

Questions

What is the Bank of Baroda statement password?

Reports differ. Your Customer ID is the format most guides agree on; the mobile number and the first 4 letters of your name plus DDMM are also reported. The statement email states the exact format.

Is the BOBCARD credit card password the same?

No. BOBCARD statements use their own name-and-date format.

Do statements I download myself have a password?

Not always. PDFs downloaded from bob World or Baroda Connect are sometimes unprotected.
