ICICI Bank Statement Password Format (2026)

ICICI Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Reported as the first four letters of the account name in lowercase followed by your day and month of birth (DDMM), or your 12-digit account number.

ICICI Bank account e-statements are reported to open with the first four letters of the account name in lowercase, followed by your day and month of birth (DDMM). For Ravi Kumar, born 15 May 1990, that would be ravi1505.

This format was published on ICICI Bank's own help site, but we could not open that page to confirm it as of October 2026, so treat it as reported, not confirmed. The email your statement arrived with states the exact format; check it first.

The password format

The format below is widely quoted from ICICI's help-centre answer "What is the password to open my e-statement?". That page was not reachable when we checked, so we have not verified the wording ourselves.

  • Savings account: first 4 letters of the account title, in lowercase, then your date and month of birth as DDMM.
  • Current account: first 4 letters of the account (business) name, in lowercase, then the date and month of incorporation as DDMM.
  • No extras: leave out spaces, special characters and salutations such as Mr or Mrs.
  • Alternative: your 12-digit account number, with its last digits shown in the email's subject line as a hint.
  • Joint accounts: use the first account holder's name and date of birth.

Some websites say the letters are in capitals, or add the birth year. The version attributed to ICICI uses lowercase and DDMM only. If one version fails, try the other, then go back to the email.

Worked example

Ravi Kumar was born on 15 May 1990 and his ICICI savings account is in the name "Ravi Kumar".

  1. First four letters of the account name, lowercase: ravi
  2. Day and month of birth as DDMM: 1505
  3. Password: ravi1505

If his name were Om Rao, the first four letters would come from "omrao" with the space removed: omra.

If the password doesn't work

  • Check the case. Try all lowercase first, then capitals.
  • Use the name as the bank has it. The account title may include a middle name or initial, or a spelling different from what you use day to day. It is printed at the top of your statement or passbook.
  • Leading zeros. 5 January is 0501, not 51.
  • Try the account number. Type the full 12-digit account number with no spaces.
  • Credit card statements are different. ICICI credit card statements have their own format; see our ICICI credit card statement guide.
  • Download it again. You can view and download statements in iMobile or internet banking; ICICI's statements page lists the ways to get one. You can also contact ICICI Bank customer care.

Questions

What is the ICICI Bank e-statement password?

It is reported to be the first four letters of the account name in lowercase followed by your birth date as DDMM, for example ravi1505. Check the statement email to confirm.

What is the password for an ICICI current account statement?

It is reported to be the first four letters of the business name in lowercase followed by the date and month of incorporation as DDMM.

Is there another password if the name format fails?

Your 12-digit account number is reported as an alternative, with its last digits shown in the email subject line.

Which holder's details do I use for a joint account?

Use the first account holder's name and date of birth.
