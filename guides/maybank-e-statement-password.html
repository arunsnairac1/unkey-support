As of October 2026, Maybank's emailed statements open with the email statement password you set in Maybank2u. If you never set one, Maybank uses your date of birth as the default. The exact date layout most often reported is ddMmmYYYY, so a made-up customer, Lee Mei Ling, born 12 March 1985, would type 12Mar1985. Maybank does not confirm that layout on its website, so check the email your statement came with.

The password format

Maybank is different from most banks. There isn't one fixed formula for everyone. Instead:

  • If you registered for email statements in Maybank2u, you chose a password then, or later under Email Statement Delivery. That password opens your statements. Maybank's e-Statement FAQ says that when registering you can "key in your preferred password or defaulted password".
  • If you used the default, the password is your date of birth. Maybank's Combined e-Statement FAQ says you can "re-register your email statement password or default it to your date of birth".

Maybank's FAQ pages don't spell out how the date should be typed. Customers commonly report it as day, short month name, full year, for example 01Jan1980. Treat that as likely, not certain.

This covers Maybank e-statements for personal savings and current accounts, loans and financing, hire purchase and individual credit cards. For supplementary credit cards on a joint statement, Maybank says only the primary cardholder can view the e-statement.

Worked example

Lee Mei Ling (made-up) was born on 12 March 1985 and never changed her email statement password. Using the commonly reported layout she would try:

  • 12Mar1985: day, then month as three letters with a capital first letter, then the full year

If she had set her own password in Maybank2u, say a phrase she picked, then only that phrase would open the file.

If the password doesn't work

  • Did you set your own password? If you ever registered for email statements in Maybank2u, the password is probably the one you chose there, not your date of birth.
  • Reset it in Maybank2u. Maybank's password FAQ says to log in at maybank2u.com.my, go to Settings, then Accounts, choose Email Statement Delivery, tap the three-dot menu, select Edit Details and enter a new password.
  • A new password only works on new statements. Maybank's email statement FAQ warns that older emailed statements still need the password that was active when they were sent.
  • Download it instead. You can view and download recent statements directly in Maybank2u under your statements, without the email password.
  • Read the email. Statements come from addresses such as m2u@stmts.maybank2u.com.my. The email usually says which password to use.
  • Not on Maybank2u? Call the Maybank contact centre on 1300 88 6688 or visit a branch.

Questions

What is the default password for a Maybank e-statement?

Your date of birth. Maybank doesn't publish the exact layout, but it is commonly reported as ddMmmYYYY, for example 12Mar1985.

How do I change my Maybank email statement password?

Log in to Maybank2u, go to Settings, then Accounts, then Email Statement Delivery, open the three-dot menu, choose Edit Details and enter a new password.

I changed my password. Why won't my old statements open?

A new password only applies to statements sent after the change. Older emails still need the password that was active when they were sent.

Can a supplementary cardholder open the Maybank e-statement?

Not on a joint statement. Maybank says only the primary cardholder can view it. If the supplementary card has a separate statement, both cardholders can view their own.
