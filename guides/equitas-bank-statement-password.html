Equitas Bank Statement Password Format (2026)

Equitas Small Finance Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Equitas savings statements are reported to open with your date of birth in DDMM followed by the first 3 letters of your name in capitals; current accounts with your PAN.

DocumentPasswordStatus
Savings account e-statementDate of birth (DDMM) + first 3 letters of name in capitals

As of October 2026, Equitas Small Finance Bank savings statements are reported to open with your date of birth in DDMM followed by the first 3 letters of your name in capitals. Kavya Iyer, born on 22 July, would type 2207KAV. Current account statements are reported to use your PAN.

The password format

Equitas does not publish the PDF password rule on its website, so the formats on this page come from independent guides that agree with each other. Treat them as commonly reported, not officially confirmed. The email or message that delivered your statement states the exact format, and it is always the one to trust.

Easemoney and ScanPilot give the same two formats:

AccountFormatExample
SavingsDDMM + first 3 letters of name, capitals2207KAV
CurrentPANABCDE1234F

Note the order: the date comes first, then the letters. Most banks do it the other way round, so this is the usual mistake.

Worked example

Kavya Iyer (made up), born 22 July 1991, savings account:

  1. Day and month: 2207
  2. First 3 letters of the name, capitals: KAV
  3. Password: 2207KAV

If the password doesn't work

  • Order. 2207KAV, not KAV2207.
  • Capitals. The letters and the PAN are uppercase.
  • Three letters, no year. Only 7 characters for savings statements.
  • Name as per bank records. Use the name Equitas holds, which may start with an initial.
  • Get a fresh copy. Easemoney reports you can generate a statement through the Equitas website chatbot or net banking.
  • Read the statement email, or call Equitas customer care.

Questions

What is the Equitas bank statement password?

For savings accounts it is reported to be your date of birth in DDMM followed by the first 3 letters of your name in capitals, like 2207KAV. Check the statement email to confirm.

What about Equitas current accounts?

Current account statements are reported to open with your PAN in capitals.

Does the date come before the name?

Yes, in the reported format the date comes first.
