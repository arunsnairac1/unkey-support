Standard Chartered UAE eStatement Password (2026)

Standard Chartered Bank (UAE) · United Arab Emirates · Updated October 2026

Credit cards: first letter of the primary cardholder's embossed name followed by date of birth as DDMMYY (e.g. D141280); bank accounts: your full 11-digit account number.

As of October 2026, Standard Chartered UAE says the default password for a credit card eStatement is the first letter of the primary cardholder's name as embossed on the card, followed by the date of birth in DDMMYY format. For example, Fatima Khan (made-up), born 3 July 1990, would type F030790. Account eStatements use your full 11-digit account number.

The password format

Standard Chartered UAE's help centre lists two default passwords:

StatementDefault passwordBank's example
Credit card (primary cardholder)First letter of name as embossed on the card + date of birth (DDMMYY)David Matthews, born 14 December 1980: D141280
Bank accountFull 11-digit account numberNone given

The date is day, month, year, two digits each, with no separators. The letter is the first letter of the name printed on the card, which is usually your first name. Standard Chartered's example uses a capital letter.

Standard Chartered's eStatements page also says statements are encrypted with a unique password "that will be provided in the eStatement email". If the email gives different instructions, follow the email.

The bank offers two services. With Email eStatement, the encrypted PDF is sent to your registered email address. With View Online, you only get an email alert and you open the statement in Online Banking. Both are available for all bank accounts and all credit cards.

Worked example

Fatima Khan (made-up) is the primary cardholder. Her card reads FATIMA KHAN, and she was born on 3 July 1990.

  • First letter of the embossed name: F
  • Date of birth as DDMMYY: 030790

Credit card password: F030790. Her current account number is 01234567890 (made-up), so her account eStatement opens with 01234567890.

If the password doesn't work

  • Did you change it? Once you change the eStatement password in Online Banking, the default no longer works.
  • Forgot your own password? Log in to Standard Chartered Online Banking and open your eStatements there. The bank says no password is needed to view them in Online Banking. You can also reset the password under eStatements > Change eStatement Settings.
  • Use the embossed name. If your card shows a different first name or an initial, use the first letter exactly as it is printed on the card.
  • Supplementary cards. The help centre describes the password for the primary cardholder. Supplementary cardholders should check the email or ask the bank.
  • Account number length. Type all 11 digits, including any leading zeros, with no spaces.
  • Update Adobe Reader. The bank says encrypted eStatements need Adobe Acrobat Reader 8.0 or later.
  • Call the bank on 600 5222 88.

Questions

What is the Standard Chartered UAE credit card statement password?

By default, the first letter of the primary cardholder's name as embossed on the card, followed by the date of birth in DDMMYY, for example D141280.

What is the password for a Standard Chartered UAE account eStatement?

By default, your full 11-digit account number.

I changed my eStatement password and forgot it. What now?

Log in to Standard Chartered Online Banking and open the eStatement there, where no password is needed. You can also set a new password under eStatements, Change eStatement Settings.

Can I change the eStatement password?

Yes. Log in to Standard Chartered Online Banking, open eStatements and choose Change eStatement Settings.
