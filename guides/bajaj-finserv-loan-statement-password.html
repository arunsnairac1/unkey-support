A Bajaj Finserv (Bajaj Finance) loan statement usually opens with your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, for example 15051990 for someone born on 15 May 1990. If that fails, try your registered mobile number, then your PAN.

As of October 2026, Bajaj Finserv's own help pages do not all give the same answer, so treat this as a short list to try. The password hint on the download screen or in the email the statement came with is the authority.

What Bajaj Finserv's pages say

Bajaj Finserv pageWhat it says
Secured loan statement"The password is generally your registered mobile number or date of birth (DDMMYYYY)."
Auto loan statement"The password is typically your Date of Birth in the DD/MM/YYYY format."
EMI statementSome statements use "your PAN or date of birth". Bajaj adds that this isn't standard for every document, so check the download screen or instructions.

The common thread is your date of birth. The words "generally", "typically" and "often" are Bajaj's own, which is why it is worth knowing the alternatives.

Worked example

Ravi Kumar, born 15 May 1990, mobile 9876504321, PAN ABCDE1234F (all made-up). Try in this order:

  1. 15051990 (date of birth, DDMMYYYY)
  2. 9876504321 (registered mobile number, 10 digits, without +91)
  3. 15/05/1990 (date of birth with slashes, as worded on the auto loan page)
  4. ABCDE1234F (PAN in capitals)

Getting the statement

You can download a loan statement from the Bajaj Finserv app or the My Account customer portal by choosing the loan and the statement option. Statements can also be emailed to your registered email address. Statements that are emailed or downloaded are usually the ones that are password-protected.

If the password doesn't work

  • Leading zeros. 5 March 1992 is 05031992, not 531992.
  • Date of birth in Bajaj's records. Use the date of birth on your loan application or KYC. If it was recorded wrongly, the password will match the wrong date.
  • Which mobile number? Use the number registered on the loan, not a new number you never updated. Leave out +91 and the leading zero.
  • Co-applicants and business loans. The password usually follows the primary borrower's details. For a business loan, check the email, which may use the company's details.
  • Read the email or screen. Bajaj says the format can differ by document, so the hint that comes with the file wins over any list.
  • Still stuck? Download the statement again from the app, or raise a request through Bajaj Finserv's customer support.

Questions

What is the password for a Bajaj Finserv loan statement?

Usually your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, for example 15051990. Bajaj Finserv's pages also mention your registered mobile number, and for some documents your PAN.

Why do Bajaj Finserv's pages give different passwords?

Bajaj says the format isn't standard for every document type. Check the download screen or the email that came with your statement for the exact format.

Do I include +91 when using my mobile number?

Use the 10-digit registered mobile number without +91 or a leading zero.

Can I get the statement without a password?

Bajaj Finserv protects statements for privacy, but once you have opened yours you can save an unlocked copy for your own use.
