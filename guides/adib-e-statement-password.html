ADIB e-Statement Password Format (2026)

Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) · United Arab Emirates · Updated October 2026

Read in: English · العربية

First 4 letters of your name (lowercase, no spaces) followed by your date of birth as DDMMYY, e.g. moha221074.

As of October 2026, Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) says the password for your account e-statement is the first 4 letters of your name, in lowercase with no spaces, followed by your date of birth as DDMMYY. For example, Omar Saeed (made-up), born 9 March 1987, would type omar090387.

The password format

ADIB announced the rule in an "Important update" notice titled Your account e-statement password requirements have been updated, published on its website as a PDF mailer. It says: key in "the first 4 letters of your name, (no spaces & lowercase), followed by the date, month and the last two digits of the year of your birth (DDMMYY)."

ADIB gives two examples:

Name and date of birthPassword
MOHAMMED HAMAD, 22-10-1974moha221074
ALI AHMED, 01-02-2001alia010201

The second example matters: when your first name has fewer than 4 letters, you carry on into the next part of your name, skipping the space. ALI AHMED becomes alia.

Worked example

Omar Saeed (made-up) was born on 9 March 1987.

  • First 4 letters of the name, lowercase: omar
  • Date of birth as DDMMYY: 090387

Password: omar090387. If his name on record were JO ANNE SMITH, the first 4 letters without spaces would be joan.

If the password doesn't work

  • Lowercase only. ADIB's examples are all lowercase. Omar090387 will fail.
  • Use your name as the bank has it. This is the name on your ADIB account, usually as on your passport or Emirates ID, not a nickname. If your record starts with a title or a different spelling (Mohammed vs Mohamed), the letters change.
  • No spaces or punctuation. Skip spaces, hyphens and apostrophes when counting the 4 letters.
  • Two-digit year. Use only the last two digits of the year, with leading zeros for day and month.
  • Older statements. ADIB described this as an updated requirement, so statements sent before the change may use an older password. Check the email each statement came with.
  • Credit cards and business accounts. The notice is about account e-statements. For card or corporate statements, follow the email instructions.
  • Call ADIB on 600 543216, the number printed on the notice.

Questions

What is the ADIB e-statement password?

The first 4 letters of your name in lowercase with no spaces, followed by your date of birth as DDMMYY, for example moha221074.

My first name is only 3 letters. What do I type?

Continue into the next part of your name without the space. ADIB's own example turns ALI AHMED into alia.

Is the ADIB password case-sensitive?

Yes. Type the letters in lowercase, as in ADIB's examples.

Does this apply to ADIB credit card statements?

ADIB's notice covers account e-statements. For credit card statements, check the instructions in the email the statement came with.
