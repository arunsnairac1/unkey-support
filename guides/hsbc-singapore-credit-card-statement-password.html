HSBC Singapore Credit Card Statement Password (2026)

HSBC Bank (Singapore) · Singapore · Updated October 2026

Abbreviated card name (first letter of each word) followed by the last 8 digits of your card number, with no spaces, e.g. HVR12345678.

As of October 2026, HSBC Singapore says your emailed credit card statement opens with the abbreviated card name followed by the last 8 digits of your card number, with no spaces. For example, Tan Wei Ming (made-up), with an HSBC Visa Revolution card ending 5501 2468, would type HVR55012468.

The password format

HSBC's e-statement FAQ says to take the first letter of each word in the card's product name, then add the last 8 digits of the card number. HSBC notes the format was changed so it no longer uses your personal information.

The abbreviations HSBC shows are in capital letters. The table above is taken from HSBC's FAQ. If your card isn't listed, apply the same rule to the product name printed on your statement email or card.

Bank accounts are different. HSBC Singapore says it no longer emails encrypted statements for current and savings accounts. You view or download them in the HSBC Singapore app or Online Banking, where statements from the last 12 months are available.

Worked example

Tan Wei Ming (made-up) has an HSBC Visa Revolution card numbered 4000 0000 5501 2468.

  • Card name: HSBC VISA REVOLUTION, so the initials are HVR
  • Last 8 digits: 55012468

His password is HVR55012468.

If the password doesn't work

  • Use capitals and no spaces. Type the initials in capitals, as HSBC shows them, with no spaces or dashes before the digits.
  • Use the right card name. Some names don't abbreviate the way you might expect. HSBC Prive uses HP and HSBC Visa Card uses HVC. Check the table.
  • Replaced card? If your card was reissued with a new number, try the last 8 digits of the card number the statement belongs to.
  • Old statements. PDFs sent before HSBC changed the format may use an older password based on personal details. HSBC's FAQ doesn't describe the old format.
  • Read the email the statement came with. It explains the password.
  • Download it instead from the HSBC Singapore app or Online Banking, or call HSBC on 1800 4722 669 (local).

Questions

What is the HSBC Singapore credit card statement password?

The first letter of each word in your card's name, followed by the last 8 digits of your card number, with no spaces. An HSBC Visa Revolution card ending 12345678 uses HVR12345678.

Does HSBC Singapore still email bank account statements?

No. HSBC says it no longer emails encrypted statements for current and savings accounts. Download them from the HSBC Singapore app or Online Banking.

Does the HSBC Singapore password use my date of birth or NRIC?

Not any more. HSBC says the current format doesn't use personal information. It uses only the card name and card number.

Should the letters be capitals?

Yes, type them in capitals as HSBC's examples show, for example HLCC for HSBC Live+ Credit Card.
