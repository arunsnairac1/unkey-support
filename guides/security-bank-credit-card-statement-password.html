As of October 2026, Security Bank says your downloaded credit card eSOA PDF opens with your date of birth in DDMMMYYYY format, with the month in capital letters. For example, Jose Reyes (made-up), born 7 February 1989, would type 07FEB1989. Corporate cards use the last 6 digits of the card number instead.

The password format

Security Bank sends a monthly text with a secure link to your electronic Statement of Account (eSOA). Its SMS eSOA page describes two different passwords, one for the link and one for the PDF:

StepAll cards except corporateCorporate cards
Opening the link (access page)OTP + date of birth (DDMMMYYYY). Bank example: 12345605NOV1980OTP + last 6 digits of card. Bank example: 123456987654
Opening the downloaded PDFDate of birth (DDMMMYYYY). Bank example: 05NOV1980Last 6 digits of card. Bank example: 123456

The OTP is the one-time password you generate on the access page. It forms the first digits of the access password. Once you have downloaded the PDF, you don't need the OTP again. Only the date of birth (or card digits) opens the file.

Security Bank says the electronic statement is usually available 3 to 5 days after your statement date, so the SMS arrives a few days after your cut-off.

Worked example

Jose Reyes (made-up) has a personal Security Bank credit card and was born on 7 February 1989.

  1. He taps the link in the SMS and generates an OTP, say 482913.
  2. On the access page he types 48291307FEB1989.
  3. He taps Download e-SOA. To open the PDF he types 07FEB1989.

His employer's corporate card ends in 3355 7712, so its downloaded eSOA opens with 557712.

If the password doesn't work

  • Capitals for the month. Security Bank's examples use NOV, not Nov. This is different from BDO, which uses Nov.
  • Two-digit day. The 5th is 05.
  • Don't include the OTP in the PDF password. The OTP is only for the web access page.
  • Limited OTP tries. The bank says you can generate up to 5 OTP requests, so type carefully.
  • Expired link. SMS links expire 30 days after you receive them. If a link won't load, try clearing your browser cache, or ask the bank for a new copy.
  • Use the Security Bank app. You can also view and download your credit card eSOA in the app.
  • Call the bank on (02) 8887-9188 if your date of birth on record may be wrong.

Removing the password so you don't have to type it again

Once the statement opens, you can keep an unlocked copy so you are not typing the password every month. You can also do the same with Preview on a Mac or the "print to PDF" option in most PDF readers. Store the unlocked copy somewhere private, because anyone who gets hold of it can read it.

Questions

What is the password for a Security Bank credit card eSOA PDF?

Your date of birth in DDMMMYYYY format with the month in capitals, for example 05NOV1980. For corporate cards, it is the last 6 digits of the card number.

What do I type on the SMS eSOA link page?

The OTP you generate on that page followed by your date of birth in DDMMMYYYY, for example 12345605NOV1980. Corporate cards use OTP plus the last 6 card digits.

How long does the Security Bank eSOA link work?

The link expires 30 days after you receive the SMS, so download the PDF before then.

How do I stop SMS eSOA?

Text STOP SBC SOASMS to 21583106 (Globe and TM) or 225653106 (other networks), or call (02) 8887-9188.
