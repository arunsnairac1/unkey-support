Indian Bank Statement Password Format (2026)

Indian Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Indian Bank account statements are commonly reported to open with your full account number, typed without spaces; the bank does not publish this on its website.

DocumentPasswordStatus

Source

As of October 2026, Indian Bank statement PDFs are commonly reported to open with your full account number, typed without spaces. If Lakshmi Devi's account number is 6012345678, her password would be 6012345678. Indian Bank does not publish this rule on its website, so check the email your statement came with.

The password format

Indian Bank's internet banking page lists account statements among its online services but does not say how the PDF is protected. Guides that cover Indian Bank statements mostly agree that the password is the complete account number, with every digit and nothing else: no spaces, no hyphens, no branch code.

One or two sources mention other patterns, such as a name and date of birth combination, but these are not widely reported. Indian Bank merged with Allahabad Bank in 2020, and statements for older Allahabad Bank accounts or from different channels (email, net banking, the IndOASIS app) may follow their own rule. When the email or download screen states a password format, follow that.

Worked example

Lakshmi Devi has an Indian Bank savings account numbered 6012345678. This number is made up.

  • Password: 6012345678
  • Not 60 1234 5678 (spaces) and not just the last few digits

If your account number is printed with spaces in your passbook, type it as one continuous string of digits.

Where to find your account number

Your account number is on the first page of your passbook, on your cheque book, at the top of any earlier statement, and on the account summary screen in Indian Bank net banking or the IndOASIS app. On a phone, it is easiest to copy it from the app and paste it into the PDF password box, which avoids typing mistakes.

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the email. The covering email or SMS is the most reliable source for your statement's password and overrides general guides like this one.
  • Use the full number. Copy the account number from your passbook or the app rather than typing from memory, and include any leading zeros.
  • Customer ID is different. Your Customer ID (CIF) is not the account number. If the account number fails, the email may refer to the CIF instead.
  • Merged accounts. If you were an Allahabad Bank customer, make sure you use your current Indian Bank account number, not the old one.
  • Several accounts. Each statement is usually locked with the number of the account it covers.
  • Still stuck? Ask your branch or Indian Bank customer care to confirm the format. The bank will never ask for your internet banking password, PIN or OTP.

Questions

What is the Indian Bank statement password?

It is commonly reported to be your full account number without spaces, but Indian Bank does not publish this on its website. The email that came with the statement states the exact rule.

Is it my account number or my Customer ID?

Most guides say the account number. If that fails, check the email, which may mention the Customer ID instead.

I was an Allahabad Bank customer. Which number do I use?

Use your current Indian Bank account number, as shown in your passbook or app after the merger.
