BDO Credit Card eSOA Password Format (2026)

BDO Unibank · Philippines · Updated October 2026

Your date of birth in Mmmddyyyy format (three-letter month, two-digit day, four-digit year), e.g. Jan081985; statements before 15 June 2025 used ddMmmyyyy, e.g. 08Jan1985.

As of October 2026, BDO says the default password for your credit card electronic Statement of Account (eSOA) is your date of birth written as Mmmddyyyy: three-letter month, two-digit day, four-digit year. For example, Maria Santos (made-up), born 14 September 1991, would type Sep141991. Statements sent before 15 June 2025 use the older order, 14Sep1991.

The password format

BDO's eSOA password advisory says that from 15 June 2025 the default password changed:

StatementsFormatBDO's example
Before 15 June 2025ddMmmyyyy08Jan1985
From 15 June 2025MmmddyyyyJan081985

Both use the same pieces, just in a different order:

  • Mmm: the month as three letters, first letter capital, the rest lowercase (Jan, Feb, Mar … Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec)
  • dd: the day as two digits, so the 8th is 08
  • yyyy: the full four-digit year

There are no spaces, dashes or slashes. The months are written Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov and Dec.

Worked example

Maria Santos (made-up) was born on 14 September 1991.

  • Her July 2026 eSOA (new format): Sep141991
  • An eSOA from March 2025 that is still in her inbox (old format): 14Sep1991

Someone born on 3 May 1978 would use May031978 now and 03May1978 for older statements.

If the password doesn't work

  • Check the statement date. If the eSOA is from before mid-June 2025, try the old day-first format. If it is newer, put the month first.
  • Capitals matter. Only the first letter of the month is a capital. SEP141991 or sep141991 won't match BDO's examples.
  • Use two digits for the day. Type 08, not 8.
  • Use the birth date BDO has on file. If your date of birth was recorded wrongly when you applied, the password follows BDO's record. Call BDO to check.
  • Much older statements. Before BDO switched to date-of-birth passwords, older eSOAs were commonly reported to use the last digits of the card number. BDO's current page doesn't cover this.
  • Read the email that came with the eSOA. It usually repeats the password format.
  • Contact BDO on (+632) 8888-0000 or callcenter@bdo.com.ph. BDO says never to share your password or card details. BDO staff won't ask for them by email.

Spotting a fake eSOA email

A real BDO eSOA email does not ask you to reply with your password, card number or one-time PIN, or to log in through a link to "verify" your account. If an email looks wrong, forward it to reportphish@bdo.com.ph. For scams, fraud or a lost or stolen card, call BDO on (02) 8888-0000.

Questions

What is the BDO credit card eSOA password?

Since 15 June 2025, it is your date of birth as Mmmddyyyy, for example Jan081985 for 8 January 1985.

Why won't my old BDO statement open with the new password?

Statements sent before 15 June 2025 use the older format, ddMmmyyyy, for example 08Jan1985.

Is the BDO eSOA password case-sensitive?

Type it exactly as BDO's examples show: capital first letter of the month and lowercase for the rest, such as Jan or Sep.

Do I use one or two digits for the day?

Two digits. The 8th is written 08, as in BDO's example Jan081985.
