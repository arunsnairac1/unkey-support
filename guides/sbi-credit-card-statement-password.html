SBI Card does not publish its statement password format on its website. As of October 2026, it is commonly reported to be your date of birth as DDMMYYYY followed by the last 4 digits of your card. For Anita Desai, born 15 May 1990, with a card ending 4321, that would be 150519904321. The exact rule is stated in the email the statement arrives with, so check that first.

The password format

SBI Card's statement and billing FAQ explains how e-statements are sent to your registered email and how to view them online, but it does not say what the PDF password is. So everything below comes from third-party guides and cardholder reports, not from SBI Card itself.

Two formats are reported:

FormatExampleStatus
DOB (DDMMYYYY) + last 4 card digits150519904321Most often reported for current statements; not confirmed by SBI Card
First 4 letters of name (caps) + DOB (DDMM)ANIT1505Reported by some guides, possibly older; not confirmed by SBI Card

Note that SBI Card (the credit card company) is separate from State Bank of India. SBI savings account statements use a different password.

Worked example

Anita Desai was born on 15 May 1990 and her SBI Card ends in 4321. Her details are made up.

  • Date of birth as DDMMYYYY: 15051990
  • Last four digits of the card: 4321
  • Most commonly reported password: 150519904321
  • If that fails, the other reported format: ANIT + 1505 = ANIT1505

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the statement email. Since SBI Card does not publish the rule online, the email is the most reliable place to find it.
  • Full year, leading zeros. The DOB part is eight digits: 5 March 1990 is 05031990.
  • Right card. If you hold more than one SBI Card, or your card was replaced, use the last four digits of the card shown on that statement.
  • Download it instead. Log in at sbicard.com or the SBI Card app to view and download statements. SBI Card's FAQ says statements for the latest 24 months are available there.
  • Email not arriving? SBI Card suggests checking spam and junk folders if the e-statement has not arrived within 72 hours.
  • Still stuck? Contact SBI Card customer care to confirm the date of birth on record.

Questions

What is the SBI Card statement password?

SBI Card does not publish it. It is commonly reported as your date of birth (DDMMYYYY) followed by the last 4 digits of your card. Check the statement email for the exact rule.

Is the SBI Card password the same as my SBI bank statement password?

No. SBI Card and State Bank of India send separate statements with different password rules.

Where can I download my SBI Card statement?

Log in at sbicard.com or the SBI Card app. SBI Card says the latest 24 months of statements are available there.

My e-statement never arrived. What should I do?

SBI Card suggests checking your spam and junk folders if it has not arrived within 72 hours, then contacting customer care.
