AIS PDF Password Format (Income Tax, 2026)

Income Tax Department, India · India · Updated October 2026

Your PAN in lower case followed by your date of birth (or date of incorporation/formation) in DDMMYYYY format, with no spaces.

The AIS PDF password is your PAN in lower case followed by your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, with no spaces. For a made-up PAN ABCPK1234L and a date of birth of 15 May 1990, the password is abcpk1234l15051990.

As of October 2026, the Income Tax Department states this format in its guide Steps to access AIS information online.

The password format

The Annual Information Statement (AIS) is downloaded from the income-tax e-filing portal (AIS tab after you log in). If you choose PDF, the file is password protected. The department's guide says the password is the combination of:

  1. Your PAN in lower case (all ten characters, letters typed small), and
  2. Your date of birth for individuals, or the date of incorporation/formation for companies, firms and other non-individuals, written as DDMMYYYY.

There is no space or symbol between the two parts. The department's own example is PAN AAAAA1234A and date of birth 21 January 1991, which gives aaaaa1234a21011991.

Worked example

Priya Sharma has the made-up PAN BQKPS4821M and was born on 7 September 1988.

  • PAN in lower case: bqkps4821m
  • Date of birth as DDMMYYYY: 07091988
  • Password: bqkps4821m07091988

For a company with PAN AAFCR5678Q incorporated on 2 April 2012, the password would be aafcr5678q02042012.

AIS JSON uses upper case

If you download AIS as a JSON file and import it into the department's offline AIS utility, the password is different: the PAN in upper case plus the same DDMMYYYY date, for example BQKPS4821M07091988. This is stated in the department's guide Steps to access AIS information offline. Mixing up the two is the most common reason the password "doesn't work".

What about TIS?

The Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS) sits next to AIS on the same dashboard (see the department's AIS/TIS FAQ). The official page we checked does not state a separate password for the TIS PDF. It is commonly reported that the TIS PDF uses the same password as the AIS PDF (PAN in lower case plus DDMMYYYY), but we could not confirm this from an official source, so try that first and check the portal if it fails.

If the password doesn't work

  • PAN letters must be small. BQKPS4821M07091988 opens the JSON, not the PDF.
  • Use two digits for day and month. 7 September is 0709, not 79.
  • Use the date on your PAN record. The date of birth the department has against your PAN is what counts, even if it differs from other documents. You can see it in your profile on the e-filing portal.
  • Non-individuals use the date of incorporation or formation, not a director's or partner's date of birth.
  • Re-download. If a file was partly downloaded, it may not open at all. Download it again from the AIS tab.

For AIS questions, the department lists the AIS and Reporting Portal helpline 1800 103 4215 on its AIS FAQ page.

Questions

What is the password for the AIS PDF?

Your PAN in lower case followed by your date of birth as DDMMYYYY, for example abcpk1234l15051990.

Why doesn't my AIS password work with capital letters?

The AIS PDF uses the PAN in lower case. The capital-letter version is for importing the AIS JSON file into the offline utility.

What is the AIS password for a company or firm?

The PAN in lower case followed by the date of incorporation or formation in DDMMYYYY format.

Is the TIS PDF password the same as AIS?

It is commonly reported to be the same (PAN in lower case plus DDMMYYYY), but the official page we checked does not say so explicitly.
