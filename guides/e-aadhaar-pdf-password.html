e-Aadhaar PDF Password Format (2026)

Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) · India · Updated October 2026

The first four letters of your name as printed on Aadhaar, in capital letters, followed by your year of birth (YYYY).

The e-Aadhaar PDF password is the first four letters of your name in capital letters, followed by your year of birth (YYYY). For example, Ravi Kumar born in 1990 would type RAVI1990.

As of October 2026, this is the rule UIDAI gives in its own FAQ, What is the password of e-Aadhaar?.

The password format

UIDAI says the password is "a combination of the first 4 letters of name in CAPITAL and the year of birth (YYYY)". In practice that means:

  • Take your name exactly as it is printed on your Aadhaar.
  • Use the first four characters, in capital letters, even if you normally write your name in lower case.
  • Add the four-digit year you were born. Not the full date, only the year.
  • No spaces before the year, and nothing else at the end.

Worked examples

UIDAI's FAQ gives four cases that cover most of the tricky names. Here they are with made-up people:

Name on AadhaarYear of birthPassword
RAVI KUMAR1990RAVI1990
SAI KIRAN1985SAIK1985 (the space is skipped and the next letter is used)
K. MEENA1978K.ME1978 (the dot counts as one of the four characters)
RIA2001RIA2001 (name shorter than four letters: use all of it)

Two details from UIDAI's examples are worth noticing. A name like "Sai Kumar" becomes SAIK: the four letters run across the space. A name with an initial and a dot, like "P. Kumar", keeps the dot: P.KU.

If the password doesn't work

  • Caps lock and case. The letters must be capitals. Ravi1990 will not work; RAVI1990 will.
  • Use the name on your Aadhaar, not your everyday name. If your Aadhaar says "Ravindra Kumar", the password starts with RAVI; if it says "R. Kumar", it starts with R.KU. Check the name on an older printout or on the myAadhaar portal.
  • Check the initial and dot. If your name begins with an initial, try it with the dot exactly where Aadhaar puts it.
  • Year, not date. Only four digits of the year. If your Aadhaar shows only a year of birth, use that year.
  • Recently updated details. If you changed your name or date of birth, a newly downloaded e-Aadhaar uses the updated details. An old file you saved earlier uses the details from that time.
  • Typing on a phone. Phone keyboards sometimes add a space or capitalise only the first letter. Type slowly, or paste the password from a notes app.

If none of this works, download a fresh copy of e-Aadhaar from UIDAI's official site (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) and try again. For help, UIDAI's helpline is 1947.

Is it safe to share e-Aadhaar?

The password exists to stop someone who finds the file from reading your Aadhaar number and address. If you are sending e-Aadhaar for KYC, send it only to the organisation that asked for it. UIDAI also offers a masked Aadhaar download that hides most of the Aadhaar number, which is often enough for KYC.

Questions

What is the password for e-Aadhaar PDF?

The first four letters of your name in capital letters followed by your year of birth, for example RAVI1990 for Ravi Kumar born in 1990.

My name has only three letters. What is my e-Aadhaar password?

Use the whole name in capitals followed by the year of birth. UIDAI's example is RIA born in 1990: RIA1990.

Does the space or dot in my name count?

A dot counts as a character (P. KUMAR becomes P.KU), but a space does not (SAI KUMAR becomes SAIK).

Is the e-Aadhaar password case-sensitive?

Yes. The letters must be capital letters, as UIDAI's format says.
