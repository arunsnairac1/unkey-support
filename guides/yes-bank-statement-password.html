Yes Bank Statement Password Format (2026)

Yes Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Read in: English · हिन्दी · தமிழ் · తెలుగు

Yes Bank account statements are reported to open with your Customer ID followed immediately by your date of birth in DDMMYYYY.

DocumentPasswordStatus
Savings/current account e-statementCustomer ID followed by date of birth (DDMMYYYY), no spaces
e.g. Customer ID 123456789, born 22 July 1988: 12345678922071988		Reported by 2 independent sources – not confirmed by the issuer
Source

As of October 2026, Yes Bank account statement PDFs are reported to open with your Customer ID followed by your date of birth in DDMMYYYY, typed together with no spaces. If Kavita Shah's Customer ID is 123456789 and she was born on 22 July 1988, the password would be 12345678922071988.

The password format

Yes Bank does not publish the PDF password rule on its website, so the formats on this page come from independent guides that agree with each other. Treat them as commonly reported, not officially confirmed. The email or message that delivered your statement states the exact format, and it is always the one to trust.

Both Easemoney and ScanPilot give the same rule: Customer ID, then date of birth as eight digits. With a 9-digit Customer ID that makes a 17-digit password.

Reports are not unanimous. One guide, TaxFetch, gives a name-based rule instead (first 4 letters of the name in capitals plus DDMM), and ScanPilot notes that some statements open with the date of birth alone. Yes Bank credit card statements are a separate product with their own format, so do not use this one for a card statement.

Worked example

Kavita Shah (made up) has Customer ID 123456789 and was born on 22 July 1988.

  • Customer ID: 123456789
  • Date of birth in DDMMYYYY: 22071988
  • Password: 12345678922071988

A long string of digits is easy to mistype on a phone. Copy the Customer ID from the Yes Bank app profile, paste it into the password box and then type the date.

If the password doesn't work

  • Check the date. Day first, then month, then the full year, with leading zeros: 5 March 1990 is 05031990.
  • Try the date of birth alone in DDMMYYYY, which some statements are reported to use.
  • Find your Customer ID. It is on your cheque book and welcome letter, and the net banking login page has a "Know your Customer ID" option.
  • Joint accounts. Use the primary holder's Customer ID and date of birth.
  • Download instead. Statements downloaded from the Yes Bank app or net banking are sometimes not protected at all.
  • Read the statement email. It states the exact format for that PDF.

Questions

What is the Yes Bank statement password?

It is reported to be your Customer ID followed by your date of birth in DDMMYYYY, with no spaces. Yes Bank does not publish this, so check the statement email.

How many characters is the Yes Bank statement password?

With a 9-digit Customer ID and an 8-digit date, it is 17 digits.

Does the same password open my Yes Bank credit card statement?

Not necessarily. Card statements use their own format, which is stated in the card statement email.
