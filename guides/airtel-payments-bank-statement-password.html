Airtel Payments Bank Statement Password (2026)

Airtel Payments Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Airtel Payments Bank statements are reported to open with your date of birth in DDMMYYYY followed by your 10-digit registered mobile number.

DocumentPasswordStatus
Account statementDate of birth (DDMMYYYY) + 10-digit registered mobile number
e.g. Born 22 July 1988, mobile 9876543210: 220719889876543210		Reported by 2 independent sources – not confirmed by the issuer
Source

As of October 2026, Airtel Payments Bank statement PDFs are reported to open with your date of birth in DDMMYYYY followed by your 10-digit registered mobile number, 18 digits with no spaces. If Rohan Yadav was born on 22 July 1988 and his mobile is 9876543210, the password would be 220719889876543210.

The password format

Airtel Payments Bank does not publish the PDF password rule on its website, so the formats on this page come from independent guides that agree with each other. Treat them as commonly reported, not officially confirmed. The email or message that delivered your statement states the exact format, and it is always the one to trust.

Easemoney and ScanPilot both give the date-then-mobile rule. One other guide (TaxFetch) says the mobile number alone; if the 18-digit version fails, try that.

Worked example

Rohan Yadav (made up), born 22 July 1988, registered mobile 9876543210:

  1. Date of birth: 22071988
  2. Mobile number: 9876543210
  3. Password: 220719889876543210

Eighteen digits is a lot to type on a phone. Type slowly, or write the string in a notes app first and paste it.

If the password doesn't work

  • Date first. The date of birth comes before the mobile number.
  • Leading zeros. Born 5 March 1990 is 05031990.
  • No +91. Use the 10-digit number only.
  • Old number. Older statements may use the mobile number registered at the time.
  • Airtel Axis credit card statements are issued by Axis Bank and follow Axis Bank's format, not this one.
  • Read the message that delivered the statement, or contact Airtel Payments Bank support in the Airtel Thanks app.

Questions

What is the Airtel Payments Bank statement password?

It is reported to be your date of birth in DDMMYYYY followed by your 10-digit registered mobile number, with no spaces. Check the statement message to confirm.

How long is the password?

18 digits: 8 for the date and 10 for the mobile number.

Does this work for the Airtel Axis Bank credit card?

No. That card is issued by Axis Bank and uses Axis Bank's statement password format.
