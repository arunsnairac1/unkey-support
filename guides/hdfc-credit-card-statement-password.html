HDFC Bank does not publish its credit card statement password on its website. As of October 2026, it is commonly reported to be the first four letters of your name in capitals followed by the last four digits of your card. For Ravi Kumar with a card ending 4321, that would be RAVI4321. The exact rule is stated in the email your statement arrives with, so check that first.

The password format

HDFC's NetBanking FAQ explains that account statement PDFs open with your Customer ID, and how to manage credit card e-statement settings, but it does not say what the credit card statement password is. The formats below come from third-party guides and cardholder reports, not from HDFC Bank.

Name (4 letters, caps) + DOB as DDMMRAVI1505Reported by some guides; not confirmed by HDFC

Do not confuse this with an HDFC savings account statement. HDFC says those open with your Customer ID, which is a different rule.

Worked example

Ravi Kumar was born on 15 May 1990 and his HDFC credit card ends in 4321. His details are made up.

  • First four letters of his name, in capitals: RAVI
  • Last four digits of the card: 4321
  • Most commonly reported password: RAVI4321
  • If that fails, the other reported variant: RAVI + 1505 = RAVI1505

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the statement email. HDFC's statement email states the rule. Because HDFC does not publish it online, this is your most reliable source.
  • Capitals. Most reports say the letters must be upper case: RAVI4321, not ravi4321.
  • Name as per bank records. Use the first four letters of the name HDFC has for you, usually as printed on the card. Initials and short first names change the four letters.
  • Right card. If you have several HDFC cards or your card was reissued, use the last four digits of the card on that statement.
  • Download it instead. Statements can be viewed and downloaded in HDFC NetBanking or the HDFC Bank app under your credit card. A downloaded copy may use a different password, or none, so read the instructions on screen.
  • Still stuck? Contact HDFC Bank credit card customer care.

Questions

What is the HDFC credit card statement password?

HDFC does not publish it online. It is commonly reported as the first 4 letters of your name in capitals plus the last 4 digits of your card, such as RAVI4321. Check the statement email for the exact rule.

Is it the same as my HDFC bank statement password?

No. HDFC says account statements open with your Customer ID; credit card statements use a different rule.

Is the HDFC card statement password case-sensitive?

Yes. Type the name letters in capitals.

Where can I download my HDFC credit card statement?

In HDFC NetBanking or the HDFC Bank app, under your credit card's statements.
