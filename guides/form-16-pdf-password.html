A Form 16 or Form 16A downloaded from TRACES opens with the deductor's TAN in capital letters, for example MUMR12345D. If your employer emailed you a Form 16 PDF, the password is whatever your employer chose, so check that email first.

As of October 2026, TRACES states the TAN rule in its Form 16 download FAQ: "The password for opening Form 16 / 16A will be your TAN in capital letters."

The password format

Form 16 (salary TDS certificate) and Form 16A (TDS on other payments) are generated on TRACES. Only the deductor, meaning your employer or the payer, can download them. TRACES says plainly that "through a taxpayer login, Form 16 cannot be downloaded". The deductor downloads the file and converts it to PDF with the TRACES PDF Generation Utility.

For that TRACES file, the password is the TAN (the 10-character Tax Deduction Account Number of the employer or payer) in capital letters. The TAN is printed on Form 16 and on your salary slips at some companies.

Employer-emailed Form 16: passwords vary

Most employees never touch TRACES. Your employer or its payroll provider sends you a Form 16 PDF by email or through an HR portal. Many employers add their own password before sending, and there is no single official format for this. Common choices include your PAN, your date of birth, or a mix of both, but each employer decides. The password, or a hint, is almost always in the email the Form 16 came with or in the HR portal's help text. If it is not, ask your HR or payroll team. Do not assume the TRACES TAN rule applies to a file your employer emailed.

Form 16B, 16C, 16D and 16E

These are different certificates that the buyer, tenant or payer downloads from TRACES (TDS on property purchase, rent, certain payments, and virtual digital assets). The TRACES password tutorial says they open with the date of birth of the buyer/tenant/payer as per PAN, in DDMMYYYY format. The Form 16B FAQ gives the example 04101976 for a buyer born on 4 October 1976.

If the password doesn't work

  • TAN, not PAN. For TRACES Form 16/16A, it is the employer's TAN, not your PAN.
  • Capital letters. Type the TAN in capitals: MUMR12345D, not mumr12345d.
  • Which TAN? If you had two employers in the year, each Form 16 opens with that employer's TAN.
  • Employer's own password. If TAN does not work on an emailed PDF, your employer has used its own password. Look at the email again or ask HR.
  • Form 16B. Use the buyer's date of birth as per PAN, with leading zeros (07091988).

Questions

What is the password for Form 16 downloaded from TRACES?

The deductor's TAN in capital letters, according to the TRACES FAQ.

What is the password for the Form 16 my employer emailed me?

It depends on the employer. The password or a hint is usually in the email or HR portal; if not, ask your HR or payroll team.

Can I download my own Form 16 from TRACES?

No. TRACES says Form 16 cannot be downloaded through a taxpayer login; only the deductor can issue it.

What is the password for Form 16B?

The buyer's date of birth as per PAN in DDMMYYYY format, for example 15051990.
