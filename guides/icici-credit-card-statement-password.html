ICICI Credit Card Statement Password (2026)

ICICI Bank · India · Updated October 2026

The last 4 digits of your ICICI Bank credit card number.

Source

As of October 2026, ICICI Bank says the password for your credit card statement PDF is the last 4 digits of your credit card number. If Meera Pillai's card number ends in 5821, her statement opens with 5821. ICICI also says you cannot change or reset this password.

The password format

ICICI Bank's own guide to viewing your credit card statement online says it "only supports the last 4 digits of your credit card number as the password for the credit card statement PDF." The same page says the password cannot be customised and cannot be reset.

That makes ICICI one of the simplest card statements to open: no name, no date of birth, just four digits. The exact rule is also stated in the email the statement arrives with, so if ICICI ever changes it, the email is the place to check first.

Worked example

Meera Pillai holds an ICICI Bank credit card numbered XXXX XXXX XXXX 5821. Her details are made up.

  • Look at the last four digits printed on the card, or in the masked number at the top of the statement email: 5821.
  • Type 5821 when the PDF asks for a password.

If Meera has an add-on card for a family member, she should use the digits of the card the statement was issued for. The masked card number in the email shows which one that is.

If the password doesn't work

  • Check which card. If you have more than one ICICI card, each statement uses its own card's last four digits. Match the masked number in the email.
  • Replaced or reissued card. If your card was replaced after loss, upgrade or expiry, the number may have changed. Try the digits of the card that was active for that statement period.
  • Old formats you may have read about. Older guides say ICICI used your name plus date of birth. ICICI's current page says only the last four digits are supported, so try those first. For very old statements, the email they came with states the rule that applied at the time.
  • Typing on a phone. Some PDF viewers add a space or switch keyboards. Type the four digits slowly with the number pad.
  • Download a fresh copy. You can view and download statements in iMobile or ICICI internet banking. A fresh download can rule out a damaged attachment.
  • Still stuck? Contact ICICI Bank customer care. They cannot change the password, but they can confirm the card number on the statement.

Is the password safe if it is only four digits?

A four-digit password is mainly there to stop casual opening if an email is forwarded by mistake. Treat the PDF as a sensitive document: it shows your spending, limit and partial card number. Avoid storing unlocked copies in shared folders.

Questions

What is the password for an ICICI credit card statement?

ICICI Bank says it is the last 4 digits of your credit card number.

Can I change my ICICI credit card statement password?

No. ICICI says the password cannot be customised and cannot be reset.

I have two ICICI cards. Which digits do I use?

Use the last four digits of the card the statement belongs to. The masked card number in the statement email tells you which card it is.

My old ICICI statement won't open with the last 4 digits. Why?

Older statements may have used a different rule, or a card that has since been replaced. Check the email that statement came with, or download it again from iMobile or internet banking.
