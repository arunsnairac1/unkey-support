Emirates NBD e-Statement Password (2026)

Emirates NBD · United Arab Emirates · Updated October 2026

As of October 2026, Emirates NBD says the password for an account e-Statement is your primary CIF ID, which is the middle 8 digits of your account number. Type the 8 digits with no spaces. Credit card e-statements are also password protected, but the bank points you to the instructions in the statement email rather than publishing the rule.

The password format

On its e-Statements page, Emirates NBD answers the question directly: "the password is the primary CIF ID" and "The CIF ID is the middle 8 digits of your account number." The CIF (customer information file) number identifies you as a customer, so the same password usually works for every account held under that CIF.

The credit card statement help page confirms card e-statements are password protected and says "You'll find instructions on your credit card statement." Read the email the statement came with for the exact rule.

Worked example

Khalid Rahman (made-up) has a savings account. His account number is 1010123456701 (made-up). Leaving out the first three and last two digits leaves the middle 8 digits, 01234567, so that is the password he types when the PDF asks for one.

Account numbers can have different lengths, so working out "the middle" by counting can be error-prone. The safest way is to look up your CIF number directly: it is shown in the Emirates NBD app or Online Banking profile, and the Customer Service Centre can tell you. Include any leading zeros.

If the password doesn't work

Emirates NBD lists three common reasons a password is rejected:

  • A typing error or a space at the end of the CIF number. Some phones add a space after autofill; delete it.
  • You don't know your correct CIF ID. Check it in the app or ask the bank rather than guessing from the account number.
  • You have several CIFs. If you hold accounts under different CIF numbers (for example a personal and a joint relationship), use the CIF of the account this statement is for.

Other things to check:

  • Use a proper PDF reader. The bank says you need Adobe Acrobat Reader 6.0 or later; on a phone, the Acrobat app works on iPhone, iPad and Android.
  • Card statements are different. The CIF rule is for account e-Statements. For a credit card statement, follow the email.
  • Call the bank. Emirates NBD's Customer Service Centre is on 600 54 0000. They will never ask for your Online Banking password.

Getting e-Statements

The bank says existing Online Banking users are enrolled by default, statements arrive by email on the same cycle as paper statements, and the service is free. Each account is subscribed separately. You can change the email address under Profile in Online Banking.

Questions

What is the password for an Emirates NBD e-Statement?

For account e-Statements, Emirates NBD says it is your primary CIF ID, which is the middle 8 digits of your account number.

Where do I find my Emirates NBD CIF number?

It appears in your Online Banking or app profile, and the Customer Service Centre on 600 54 0000 can confirm it. Include any leading zeros.

What is the Emirates NBD credit card statement password?

The bank doesn't publish it on its website; it says the instructions are on your credit card statement email. Follow those.

Why does my CIF number not open the statement?

The bank says the usual causes are a typo, a space after the number, or using the CIF of a different account if you have more than one.
