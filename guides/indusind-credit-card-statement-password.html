IndusInd Credit Card Statement Password (2026)

IndusInd Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Commonly reported as the first 4 letters of the name on your card followed by your date of birth as DDMM; IndusInd does not publish this for cards, so check the statement email.

Source

IndusInd Bank does not publish its credit card statement password on its website. As of October 2026, it is commonly reported to be the first four letters of the name on your card followed by your date of birth as DDMM. For Anjali Verma, born 7 November 1993, that would be ANJA0711 (some cardholders report lower case, anja0711). The exact rule is stated in the email your statement arrives with.

The password format

We could not find an IndusInd page that states the credit card statement password. Third-party guides and cardholder reports agree on the structure, name letters plus day and month of birth, but disagree on whether the letters are capitals or lower case.

For comparison, IndusInd's own account statement email uses the same structure for savings and current accounts: "first 4 characters of your name and your date of birth in the DDMM format", case-sensitive, with examples in capitals. That page is about bank accounts, not cards, so it supports but does not confirm the card format.

Worked example

Anjali Verma was born on 7 November 1993 and her card shows ANJALI VERMA. Her details are made up.

  • First four letters of the name on the card: ANJA
  • Day and month of birth: 0711
  • Try ANJA0711 first, then anja0711

If the password doesn't work

  • Read the statement email. It states the rule for your card, including whether letters are capitals.
  • Try both cases, once each. Then stop guessing and check the email.
  • Name as embossed. Use the name printed on the card. If it starts with an initial, the initial is usually part of the four characters.
  • DDMM only. No year, and keep leading zeros: 7 November is 0711.
  • Bank account statement instead? IndusInd account statements also accept your 8-digit Customer ID as an alternative password, per the bank's statement email. That alternative is documented for accounts, not cards.
  • Download it instead. Statements can be viewed in the IndusInd mobile app or internet banking, or contact customer care.

Keep the statement safe

Your IndusInd statement shows your spending, credit limit and part of your card number. A password made from your name and birthday is easy for people who know you to work out, so treat it as light protection. Never share your card PIN or OTP with anyone who calls or messages claiming to be the bank.

Questions

What is the IndusInd credit card statement password?

IndusInd does not publish it for cards. It is commonly reported as the first 4 letters of the name on your card plus your date of birth as DDMM. Check the statement email for the exact rule.

Capitals or lower case?

Reports differ. Try capitals first, then lower case, then check the statement email.

Does the year of birth go in the password?

No. The commonly reported format uses only the day and month.

Is it the same as the IndusInd account statement password?

The structure is similar, but IndusInd only documents it for bank accounts. Follow the instructions in your card statement email.
