Karur Vysya Bank (KVB) Statement Password (2026)

Karur Vysya Bank · India · Updated October 2026

Karur Vysya Bank statements are reported to open with your Customer ID; one guide reports Customer ID plus date of birth (DDMMYYYY).

As of October 2026, Karur Vysya Bank (KVB) statement PDFs are reported to open with your Customer ID, typed exactly with no spaces. If Senthil Kumar's Customer ID is 881234567, the password would be 881234567.

The password format

KVB does not publish the PDF password rule on its website, so the formats on this page come from independent guides that agree with each other. Treat them as commonly reported, not officially confirmed. The email or message that delivered your statement states the exact format, and it is always the one to trust.

Easemoney and ScanPilot both report the Customer ID. ScanPilot suggests two fallbacks: your date of birth in DDMMYYYY, then the last 4 digits of the account number plus DDMM. A third guide, TaxFetch, reports Customer ID followed by DDMMYYYY.

Worked example

Senthil Kumar (made up) has Customer ID 881234567, account ending 6789, and was born on 22 July 1988. In order:

  1. 881234567 (Customer ID)
  2. 22071988 (date of birth)
  3. 67892207 (last 4 of account + DDMM)
  4. 88123456722071988 (Customer ID + DDMMYYYY)

If the password doesn't work

  • Customer ID, not account number. They are different numbers.
  • Find your Customer ID. It is on the first page of your passbook, in your welcome kit and in the KVB DLite app. Easemoney reports a missed-call service from your registered mobile that sends it by SMS.
  • Download it instead. Statements viewed in KVB net banking or DLite may not need a password.
  • Read the statement email, which states the format for that file, or ask your branch.

Questions

What is the KVB statement password?

It is reported to be your Customer ID. KVB does not publish the format, so check the statement email.

Where is my KVB Customer ID?

On your passbook's first page, in the welcome kit, and in the KVB DLite app and net banking.

What if the Customer ID does not work?

Try your date of birth in DDMMYYYY, then the last 4 digits of your account number plus DDMM, then Customer ID plus DDMMYYYY.
